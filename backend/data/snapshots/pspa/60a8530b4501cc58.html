Income Generation Scheme for PWDs

Breadcrumb

  1. Home

As per its mandate, the Authority has designed programs, formulate policies, suggest interventions and provide guidance to Government departments and agencies for safeguarding the rights of the poor and the vulnerable, besides ensuring equitable allocation of resources. Along with conditional and unconditional cash assistance programs SPA has also initiated graduation schemes that serve as "exist strategy" of Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) from unconditional cash transfer support.

To engage the PWDs in Punjab in meaningful pursuit of livelihood, SPA in collaboration with Punjab Small Industries Corporation (PS|C) has launched an Income Generation Scheme for PWDs'Income Generation Scheme through Interest Free Loans for Marginalized Groups in Punjab" The scheme aims to provide interest free loans to the people with disabilities in Punjab to establish their small business to generate income on sustainable basis.

PWDs of Punjab province can achieve self-sufficiency through micro enterprises backed by interest-free loans. Under the program, all PWDs in Punjab are eligible for interest-free loans to start their own enterprises. The Punjab Small Industries Corporation (PSIC) manages the program's funds, while "Akhuwat" administers it as a service provider. Under this scheme average interest free loans ranging from Rs. 25,000 to 100,000 are being provided to the PWDs and since 2017 Rs. 270 million (revolving effect) have been disbursed. The recovery rate is 100%.

The Punjab Social Protection Authority (PSPA), established under the PPSA Act 2015, functions under the Punjab Planning & Development Board. Its mandate is to provide an efficient and inclusive social protection system for the poor and vulnerable in Punjab by developing sector-specific policies and coordinating with federal and provincial social protection organizations.
