Healthcare Benefits

The healthcare benefits include comprehensive medical treatment in the Social Security Hospitals. The facilities not available in these medical outlets are arranged through other hospitals at the cost of Punjab Employees Social Security Institution (PESSI) wherever those be available. In case the required facilities are not available within the country, the same are managed abroad to cater health requirements of the secured persons.

The healthcare benefits provided by PESSI are:

  • Complete treatment of heart diseases with bypass surgery, angiography, angioplasty etc. at Rehmatul-Lil-Alameen Institute of Cardiology, Multan Road, Lahore
  • CT scan facility at Social Security Teaching Hospital, Multan Road, Lahore
  • Nuclear Block facility for complete physical scanning at Social Security Teaching Hospital, Multan Road, Lahore
  • Diagnosis and complete treatment of breast cancer at Social Security Hospital, Multan Road, Lahore and Social Security Hospital, Islamabad
  • Complete treatment of kidney diseases in Social Security Hospitals
  • Diagnosis and treatment of gastroenteritis in all Social Security Hospitals
  • Complete treatment of hepatitis clinics in Social Security Hospitals
  • Provision of prostheses and spectacles to secured workers and their families
  • In case of non-availability of medical facilities in the country, treatment facility in abroad
  • 24/7 ambulance service with modern equipment in dispensaries and hospitals
  • Facilitation at modern laboratories and physiotherapy centers in Social Security Hospitals
  • Dengue fever diagnosis and treatment (free-of-cost) for secured workers in all Social Security Hospitals and Dispensaries
