Commissioner's Visit to Sahiwal - Review of Healthcare Facilities and Recovery Targets

Jun 13, 2026

Commissioner Punjab Employees Social Security Institution Zulfiqar Ali Kharal visited the Social Security Hospital Sahiwal, Habibabad Dispensary and Directorate of Social Security Sahiwal. During the visit, a detailed review was conducted regarding healthcare facilities provided to workers, recovery targets and field operations.

The Commissioner Punjab Social Security inspected various departments of Social Security Hospital Sahiwal including wards, OPD, emergency, laboratory and other sections in detail. Availability and functioning of medical equipment and facilities required for patient treatment were reviewed. Stock of medicines, supply system and relevant records were also checked.

He directed the Director Works to prepare a comprehensive report for increasing the number of beds in hospitals to further improve healthcare services.

During the visit, cleanliness, overall administrative matters and attendance of doctors and paramedical staff were also reviewed. The Commissioner met patients and their attendants and obtained feedback regarding medical facilities, availability of medicines and behavior of staff.
He instructed that diet charges of discharged patients must be paid immediately. He also emphasized that doctors and medical staff should maintain polite and professional behavior with patients and their families.
Later, the Commissioner visited Habibabad Dispensary where attendance registers, medicine stock, availability of medicines and other administrative records were reviewed. The Director Medical briefed the Commissioner about the performance of the dispensary.
The Commissioner directed that quality, timely and dignified healthcare must be ensured for workers and their families, and no negligence will be tolerated in the performance of duties.
Afterwards, he also visited the Directorate of Social Security Sahiwal where a detailed review of social security contribution and Workers Welfare Fund recoveries was conducted.
The concerned Directors briefed the Commissioner on recovery targets and ongoing matters. Progress of the Maryam Nawaz Ration Card Program, complaint redressal system and registration of new workers were also reviewed.
He directed that field monitoring should be further strengthened, inspection activities should be accelerated, and all recovery targets must be completed by the end of financial year. 
He stated that, in line with the vision of Chief Minister Punjab Maryam Nawaz, the benefits of welfare projects for workers must reach the grassroots level. He further emphasized that any negligence or lack of interest in achieving recovery targets will not be tolerated.
The Commissioner added that the performance of directorates is being continuously monitored, and all officers must perform their duties professionally and take effective steps to improve overall departmental performance.
Medical Advisor Dr. Humaira Buzdar, Director C&B Rashid Badar, Director Procurement Dr. Haroon and other concerned officers accompanied the Commissioner during the visit
