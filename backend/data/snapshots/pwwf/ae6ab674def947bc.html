Administration & Welfare

Welfare grants are available to workers who are employees of an industrial / Mine / Establishment must be registered under relevant Act / Law or those organizations paying 2% contribution under section 4 of the Workers Welfare Fund Act, 2019 or 5% left out contribution under the Companies Profit (Workers Participation) (Amendment) Ordinance, 2020 and must be covered under definition of workman prescribed in Punjab Industrial Relation Act PIRA-2010 and having at least 3 years accumulative service. For intermediate level, no service limit is required.  The following grants are available:

  • Talent Scholarship
  • Marriage Grant
  • Death Grant
