Eligibility Criteria for Industrial/Mining Workers

  • Industrial/Mining Worker must be covered under definition of “Worker” prescribed in Punjab Industrial Relations Act (PIRA), 2010 and must be registered with EOBI or Social Security and having at least 3 years accumulative service. However, in case of education till Intermediate level, one year service limit is required.
  • The worker must be registered with Employees old Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) or Punjab Employees Social Security Institution (PESSI), having at least nine months contribution of EOBI or PESSI out of last twelve months at his credit at the time of submission of the application.
  • Industrial/Mining Worker who are employees of an Industrial/Mine/Establishment must be registered under Factories Act, 1934 or Punjab Mining Concession Rules, 2002.
  • The children of deceased Industrial/Mining Worker of Workers Welfare Fund are eligible for Scholarship facility if his/her Widow/Legal Heirs have already been received Death Grant from Punjab Workers Welfare Fund.
  • Industrial/Mining Worker who become differently abled during service may avail Scholarship for their children, subject to the condition that the different ability is proved through a Legal Medical Board constituted for this purpose.
  • In case of retired / laid off worker, his / her fresh life certificate attested by the Government Gazetted Officer shall be furnished with the application form for scholarship case.
  • Application can be submitted within one year from the date of admission. And, in case of genuine reasons / justifications, the applicant can submit application / appeal (alongwith the supporting documents) in name of the Secretary / CEO, PWWF  for condonation of delay period which will be upto 6-months maximum.  No claim shall be entertained after the completion of the course / degree.

b.

Following fixed amounts shall be admissible for all eligible disciplines:-

  1. Boarding charges (Hostel+Mess) @ Rs.10,000/- per month.
  2. Boarding charges shall be provided only to those applicants/students whose educational institutions are located in districts other than their district of permanent address/ domicile.
  3. Boarding charges shall not be provided for intermediate-level students of private institutions and self-worker students of all levels.
  4. Transport charges @ Rs.5,000/- per month.
  5. Transport charges shall only be provided to non-boarders/ day scholars, only to those applicants / students whose educational institutions are located in district of their permanent address/ domicile.
  6. Boarding and Transport charges shall not be provided for holiday-based / weekend based / distance learning programms.

The above fee limit / capping shall be applicable on new admissions after 01.09.2025. However, boarding and transport charges mentioned at above shall be applicable on existing and new admissions for the claims submitted after 01.09.2025.

  • After Successful Registration/Sign-up, Login with the password you received through your email you entered while registration.
