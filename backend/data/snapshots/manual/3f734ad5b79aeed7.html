Eligibility Criteria

1st Semester / 1 Year Students (Fall 2026)

  • No Age Limit
  • Have domicile of Punjab province
  • No Gender specific
  • Monthly Family income of the applicant must be less than Rs. 350,000/- for Public Sector HEIs and Rs. 500,000 for Federal / Private Sector HEIs (Affidavit on E-Stamp Required) (Download Sample Affidavit)
  • The applicant must be enrolled in the 1st semester/year of Selected Disciplines in the Selected Universities / Colleges in 2026. Students already enrolled in 1st year of MBBS and BDS in 2026 are also eligible for the scholarship program.

2nd Semester / Year Onward

  • No Age Limit
  • Have domicile of Punjab province
  • No Gender specific
  • Monthly Family income of the applicant must be less than Rs. 350,000/- for Public Sector HEIs (Affidavit on E-Stamp Required) (Download Sample Affidavit)
  • The Applicant must be enrolled in 2nd semester / year onward in selected Disciplines in the selected Public Sector Universities, Medical & Dental Colleges and HED Colleges.

Frequently Asked Questions

A few answers to help you get started.

View all FAQs
01Who is eligible to apply for the Honhaar Scholarship Program?

Students domiciled in any district of Punjab who meet the prescribed academic, financial, and enrollment criteria are eligible to apply. Phase-IV covers the following categories;

  • 1st Semester / 1st Year Students (Fall 2026): Students enrolled in selected disciplines at selected public sector universities, Public Sector Medical & Dental Colleges, HED colleges, and partner Federal/Private Higher Education Institutions (HEIs).
  • 2nd Semester / Year Onward Students: Students enrolled in selected disciplines at eligible public sector universities, public sector medical and dental colleges, and HED colleges.

Applicants must fulfill all applicable eligibility requirements.

02What is the last date to apply for Phase-IV?

The last date for submission of online applications is November 09, 2026. Applicants are advised to complete and submit their applications well before the deadline.

03Can students from private universities apply for the Honhaar Scholarship?

Yes. Students enrolled in the 1st semester/1st year (Fall 2026) in selected disciplines at partner Federal/Private HEIs are eligible to apply, subject to fulfillment of the prescribed criteria. The eligibility of students enrolled in the 2nd semester/year onward is limited to the public sector institutions specified under Phase-IV.

05Are students from HED colleges eligible to apply?

Yes. Students enrolled in selected disciplines at eligible HED colleges may apply, subject to the prescribed academic requirements:

1st Semester / 1st Year: At least 65% marks in Sciences and 60% in Arts and Social Sciences.

2nd Semester / Year Onward: At least 70% equivalent GPA/CGPA in Sciences and 65% in Arts.
